# Findings

The highlights doc. Anything you noticed that might matter, in the order you
noticed it. One entry per observation, newest at the bottom.

This file is committed; the notebooks that produced it are not. Entries here are
**not claims** — they carry no interval, no control, and no guarantee anyone can
reproduce them. Their job is to stop a real observation evaporating between the
afternoon you saw it and the week you could have used it.

An entry earns promotion to `experiments/` when you would be annoyed to discover
it was noise. It earns a row in `CLAIMS.md` only after that.

## Template

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — one line, stated as what you saw

**Saw:** what appeared on screen. The literal observation.
**Guess:** what you think it means. Explicitly a guess.
**Cheapest disconfirmer:** the fastest thing that would kill the guess.
**Status:** open | promoted to eNN_slug | dropped (why)
```
